[[Têthra]], otherwise known as the tidal moon, is the closer white moon of Erodas. It completes a full lunar cycle every 64 days, and it is the moon seen over Erodas on most nights.

This moon is responsible for tidal shifts around Erodas and is often more associated with the [[Tidemother]]. Many followers of the [[Tidemother]] believe her heart exists on [[Têthra]].

The moon has a sister, [[Pêpra]], known to bring chaos and fear to folks around Erodas. When [[Pêpra]] and [[Têthra]]'s cycles align, the land and sea erupt in chaotic madness.
