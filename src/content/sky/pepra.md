[[Pêpra]], otherwise known as the lucid moon, is a smaller yellow moon that completes its cycle every 200 days. It is often concealed behind the large white moon of [[Têthra]], so typically only [[Têthra]] is visible at night.

During the full cycle of [[Pêpra]], strange things are often reported, often attributed to tricks played by the goddess [[Alune]], whom many devout followers believe resides on [[Pêpra]]. It is often cautioned for travelers to stay put, or they will witness unspeakable horrors and beasts in the night, laughing and dancing.

When [[Pêpra]] and [[Têthra]]'s cycles align, the land and sea erupt in chaotic madness.
