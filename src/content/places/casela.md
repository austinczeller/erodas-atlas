[[Casela]] is thought to be the birthplace of the gods, as the beings who inhabit these lands are so powerful.

The common intelligent species are giants and goliaths. The region shares a border with [[Dracofold]].

Little else is known of [[Casela]] in [[Korlornium]]. The Erodian Atlasing Society will update this entry as accounts come to light.
