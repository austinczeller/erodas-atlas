[[Saugeo]] is a town resembling the high desert, in the foothills of the [[Agite Mountains]]. If ya look at a scoundrel wrong they may challenge you to a Quick Draw.

The town is made up of refugees from a mining disaster in the [[Agite Mountains]] at a site operated by Queen Gold.
