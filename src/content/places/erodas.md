[[Erodas]] is the earth-like planet which contains many continents, such as [[Korlornium]]. The people of [[Erodas]] are called Erodians. [[Erodas]] exists within the Mundane World, the material plane, which exists in parallel to the Feywild and the Underdark.

## The continents

- [[Korlornium]], a small, isolated island continent, divided both physically and politically. Its history is the focus of this atlas.
- [[Goghdor]], known as the Reach, a single colossal fjord rising from the center of the Oregal Ocean, home to the Star Touched.
- [[Seivara]], a distant continent of ancient jungle, nature spirits, elves and pixies.
- [[Dracofold]], a volcanic continent northwest of [[Korlornium]], the birthplace of all dragonkind and ruled by the dragon queen Tiamat.
- [[Casela]], a large and dangerous continent home to giants, and thought to be the birthplace of the gods.

The Oregal Ocean is a vast ocean surrounding many of the most inhabited continents in [[Erodas]], such as [[Korlornium]] and [[Dracofold]].

## The sky

[[Erodas]] has two moons, but typically only [[Têthra]] is visible at night. [[Pêpra]], the yellow moon, is often concealed behind the large white moon of [[Têthra]]. Each summer [[The Astral Volley|the Astral Volley]] fills the night with falling light, and on clear nights the [[Spectral Aurora]] can be seen in the north.

## Time

The calendar year follows the seasons, but for most of history months were not distinguished from one another. Erodians simply counted the number of days they were within a season, typically 80. Calendars across [[Erodas]] have always been very region specific, so the Erodian Atlasing Society developed the [[Calendar|Erodian Standard Calendar]], a standardized method of telling the time which can be translated across continents.

## The gods

The [[Gods|Pantheon of Major Powers]] are the divine powers of current reign over the Mundane World and [[Erodas]]. It is believed that they reside in Yygarissil, the place the gods ascend to, and that as they travel within it, it creates natural phenomena which ripple onto [[Erodas]].
