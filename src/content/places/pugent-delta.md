The [[Pugent Delta]] is a region in [[Caldrith]] famous for good fertile land, with many small towns scattered across the region.

The Ossaline River, a milky white, slow river, runs through the delta from the [[Scalecrest Bluffs]] through [[Stormveil]]. The river is known to be perilous to cross as water levels fluctuate rapidly.
