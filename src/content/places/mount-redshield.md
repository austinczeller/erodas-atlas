[[Mount Redshield]] is a large volcano located in northern [[Korlornium]], where the land still joins [[Caldrith]] in the west to [[Siroth]] in the east.

During [[The Great Wave]], [[Mount Redshield]] erupted, killing many of those who dwelled in the mountains. Molten rock spilled down its slopes while a massive plume of soot and ash blotted out the sky. Before its eruption, the brilliant white stone of [[Dithoria City]] was quarried from its rock.

[[Forgas]], god of fire and forge, is said to slumber beneath the mountain, his fiery breath fueling its heart. In [[Korlornium]] people have claimed to see visions of Yygarissil, the place the gods ascend to, particularly near [[Mount Redshield]] during Winter's Crest.
