[[Wyegate]] is a town on the plains of [[Caldrith]], along the coast of [[The Scar]] just south of [[Dithoria City]]. The farmland between the two feeds much of the city.

[[Wyegate]] is best known as the site of the Battle of Wyegate during [[The Divergent War]], where the Righteous Banner, led by Hethul, the Righteous, pushed the troops of the Rellian Dynasty out of [[Caldrith]].

## Notable places

- **The Silvertree Conservatory**, an elvish school in the countryside of [[Wyegate]] that teaches half-elves the proper Elven culture.
