[[Duskwind Landing]] is a newer, small port town located on the Duskwind River in the [[Pugent Delta]] region of [[Caldrith]]. It is fairly transient, as many people are stopping and going for shipments of cargo and resupply. The vibe is a rainy port town, fairly industrial, with a shady side.

The town was notably plagued with the curse of the [[Sea Rot]], which halted export and import of goods to the harbor. A travelling band of adventurers recently quelled the curse and goods have now begun to flow. The most notable export is the trade of Spice, a deep orange powder smoked in a pipe that is common in the fields of piracy.

## Notable places

- **The Sunken Sail** and **the Rusty Hook**, the town's bars
- **The General** and **the Duskwind Fish Market**
- **The Tidemother Temple of Duskwind**
- **Sapphire Beach**, a nice tourist beach with a great view of Widow's Rook, popular with romantics for a picnic
- **The Driftwood Forest**
