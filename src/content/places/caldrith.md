[[Caldrith]] is the western side of the continent of [[Korlornium]]. Caldrith is a forested and mountainous region with scattered wetlands. City centres such as [[Dithoria City]] and [[Konduit]] make up much of the region's population. Southwest of [[Konduit]] is sparsely populated, full of muskegs and bogs, while the northern expanse is mountainous with scattered small communities typically centered around resource extraction.

Much of [[Caldrith]]'s northeastern land is still controlled by the Righteous Banner, the military brigade of [[Dithoria City]], except for the free city of [[Konduit]].
