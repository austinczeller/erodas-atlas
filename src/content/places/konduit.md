At the base of [[Mount Redshield]], [[Konduit]] is a historic city that has seen hardship. During the events of [[The Red Storm]] much of [[Konduit]] was destroyed. Mages were able to salvage important cultural buildings such as the Tower of Ancients.

Present day [[Konduit]] is a religious and political neutral point between [[Dithoria City]] and [[Siroth]]. It is considered the political center of [[Korlornium]], and it is the only city in northeastern [[Caldrith]] that is not controlled by the Righteous Banner.
