[[Thalmyre]] is a large mystical island in the center of [[The Scar]]. This island is said to be protected by the wrathful beasts born from the [[Tidemother]]. Occasionally brave pirates have attempted to find the bounties of the Dread Pirate, Captain Garrick Hollowmaw, rumored to be located on one of the islands of [[Thalmyre]]. Although [[Thalmyre]] hosts a thriving community, it is considered a dangerous lifestyle with safety not guaranteed even with strong allegiances.

The isle is controlled by the Hydra, a piracy and crime syndicate which majorly controls shipping in [[Korlornium]]. The island is home to one large port city referred to as the Seat of Thalmyre, but many of the pirate gangs have their own small communities throughout the islands, and are often in armed conflict with one another.

[[Thalmyre]] hosts [[Tidescrest]], a festival and tournament to recruit newcomers to the piracy gangs, and is where the [[Festival of Tides]] originated.

## Isles of Thalmyre

Itchy Finger, Sharktit, Floatbelly, Coffin Rock, the Seat of Thalmyre, Knife Gap, Gulper Island, Alcova, Fishhead Island, Paradise, Bent Peter and Stubb.

## Known piracy groups

Murky Bellows, the Drifting Bastards, the Rumskinners, the Gatorpaw Gang, the Rattails, the Blackwake Company and the Stray Dogs.
