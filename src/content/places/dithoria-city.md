[[Dithoria City]], named after the god of law and order, [[Dithoria]], stands as a gleaming bastion of civilization and structure amidst a chaotic world. It is the largest city in [[Korlornium]] and boasts military might across [[Caldrith]]. It was said to be blessed and protected by [[Dithoria]] during the cataclysmic event that created [[The Scar]]. While much of the surrounding land was torn asunder, the city's Silverstone walls remained unscathed, its people sheltered by the divine will of their patron god.

## Architecture and appearance

[[Dithoria City]] is a marvel of orderly design and immaculate stonework, built from Silverstone, brilliant white stone quarried from the nearby [[Mount Redshield]] before its eruption and imbued by [[Dithoria]]. The city rises in tiered levels, each section built upon the one below. Broad staircases and winding pathways connect the city's layers, leading from the bustling lower districts to the grand citadel at its peak.

[[Dithoria City]] stands on a hill at the foot of the mountains below [[Mount Redshield]], raised higher by Edric Brightbar's Silverstone terraces against [[The Great Wave]]. The farmland of the plains to the south, toward [[Wyegate]], feeds much of the city.

## The four rings

The city is divided into four rings, numbered from the top down. Each ring is divided into sections lettered clockwise from the Sterling Gate, so an address in the city is written as a ring and section, such as 3-C. Officially the rings are only numbered, but every citizen knows them by their common names.

**The First Ring, "the Citadel."** The highest ring of the city, a single section, 1-A. It is home to the Citadel of Order, the Keeper's Council and the High Priesthood. No one may enter the First Ring without a permit.

**The Second Ring, "the Coin Ring."** Sections 2-A through 2-D. It is home to the Honored House, the wealthy families of the city, and the Marble Market. Anyone may visit the Second Ring, but it takes a lot of money to do anything there.

**The Third Ring, "the Middle."** Sections 3-A through 3-F. It is home to the city's artisans and middle class, and to the Mortar Gardens.

**The Fourth Ring, "the Wash."** Sections 4-A through 4-H, the lowest ring of the city. It is home to the Sterling Gate, which sits at section 4-A, and the city's harbor on [[The Scar]]. Outsiders who come to the city spend most of their time here.

## The founding

Edric Brightbar was a half-orc and the first champion of [[Dithoria]]. He is known as the founder of [[Dithoria City]]. He built the walls of the city from Silverstone, rock of [[Mount Redshield]] imbued by [[Dithoria]], to protect the people of the plains from [[The Great Wave]].

Many statues of Edric Brightbar stand throughout the city. His iconic pose shows him with his hammer, placing the final brick of the city's wall.

## The Honored House

The Honored House, otherwise known as the Holy House, are the direct descendants of Edric Brightbar. The house is a part of the Keeper's Council and acts as the religious head of [[Dithoria]]. It is recognized as the most powerful group in [[Korlornium]] and holds immense say among the elected council, as many elected officials are religious appointees. The house lives in the Second Ring and is led by Malroy Brightbar.

## Law, magic and permits

There is a strict limitation of the use of magic within the walls of the city. The Keeper's Council writes the laws governing magic and the Arcane Order of Dithoria enforces them. Only wizards certified by the Arcane Order may practice magic on his holy land.

The council states these restrictions are for public safety. The church teaches that all magic is the power of [[Dithoria]], and that it should only be wielded by the worthy, as in the hands of the unholy it corrupts. Most people no longer believe this, but some fundamentalists still do. This has created a division in the city's society, as the control of magic is heavily regulated.

Nearly everything in [[Dithoria City]] requires a permit. Every business must display its permits, and citizens are often asked to show their identification and personal permits to the Arcane Order.

**The Keeper's Council** is a collection of powerful people within the city's government. It is made up of devoted magistrates and clerics responsible for maintaining the city's laws and societal order, and it is housed in the Citadel of Order alongside the High Priesthood.

**The Arcane Order of Dithoria** acts as the police of the city. They check the permits of citizens and businesses, enforce the laws written by the Keeper's Council, and investigate the wrongful use of magic. The order teaches that magic can corrupt the unholy, and that the power of [[Dithoria]] should only be used by the worthy.

## Notable places

### The Citadel of Order

At the highest point of the city lies the Citadel of Order, a massive fortress and temple devoted to [[Dithoria]]. Its spires and parapets gleam in the sunlight, symbolizing the unwavering strength and purity of order. The citadel houses both the High Priesthood and the Keeper's Council.

### The Sterling Gate

The main entrance to the city, heavily fortified and guarded, in the Fourth Ring.

### The Marble Market

The Marble Market is the most prestigious market in the city, known for its orderly rows and rare goods. It mainly serves the upper class, or the middle class when they want something fancy. Prices are higher here than anywhere else in the city, but so is the quality, as every product coming into or going out of the market is regulated by the Keeper's Council.

The market floor is laid in a perfect grid of white and grey marble, and every stall sits inside its own square. Merchants who let their goods spill past the line of their square are fined. The market opens and closes with the bells of the Citadel of Order, and a stall still selling after the last bell is shut down for a week.

Stalls are held by quality, not by price. Each season the goods of every stall are inspected by the Keeper's Council. If a merchant's goods do not meet the council's quality, they lose their stall and it is opened to applicants.

- **The High Row** is closest to the Citadel stair. Silks, spices, and the most expensive stalls in the city.
- **The Sealed Row** is the only place enchanted goods can legally be sold. Every item must carry the stamp of the Arcane Order, and unstamped goods are contraband.
- **The Outer Row** is closest to the Sterling Gate. Merchants from outside the city, especially from [[The Scar]], may only sell here, and they pay a higher tariff.

Haggling is considered disorder. Prices are written on slate boards each morning and can't change until the next day. All goods sold by weight must be weighed at the Scale House at the center of the market, on scales kept by the council, and cheating the scales is treated as a crime against [[Dithoria]], not against the buyer. A statue of Edric Brightbar stands beside the Scale House. Merchants rub his hammer for luck before the first bell, and it has been worn shiny.

### The Mortar Gardens

The Mortar Gardens is a park and gathering place in the Third Ring, with beautiful oak trees and grassy knolls. At its center is a monument where Edric Brightbar laid the final brick of the city's wall. It has a beautiful view of the sea and [[Mount Redshield]].

## The Righteous Banner

The Righteous Banner is the military brigade rallied during [[The Divergent War]], blessed by [[Dithoria]] to keep his kingdom safe. It defeated much of the Rellian troops at the Battle of [[Wyegate]], commanded by Hethul, the Righteous, a mighty goliath warrior who wielded the Lawbearer's Hammer. The hammer is held today by the Keeper's Council. The Righteous Banner still controls much of [[Caldrith]]'s northeastern land, except for the free city of [[Konduit]].

## Culture and society

The people of [[Dithoria City]] are known for their discipline, adherence to laws, and pride in their heritage. They believe their survival from [[The Great Wave]] was not merely good fortune, but a testament to their faith and righteousness. As such, the city's governance is steeped in religious tradition, with most legal matters presided over by priests of [[Dithoria]].

At [[Nhaalthurn]], the silence of the Quiet Evening is largely ceremonial and frequently broken. The upper classes host wordless dinner parties, communicating through written notes and dramatic gestures, and the morning celebration involves hired orchestras. During [[The Astral Volley]], the city's scholars record the shower in precise detail each year and maintain archives going back centuries.

There have been rumors of secret underground temples and places of worship to [[The Queen of Ravens]] in the holy city, as people need comfort in the trying times.

## Relations with the outside world

While the city welcomes travelers and merchants, there is an undercurrent of suspicion towards outsiders, particularly those from [[The Scar]]. The city's insular and evangelical nature has led to friction with other territories, especially those who chafe under what they perceive as [[Dithoria City]]'s moral superiority and rigid customs.

Dithorian elites have recently opened a temple to [[Dithoria]] in [[Cragbrook]], which has been met with animosity among locals. The Fowlmen in particular are opposed to westerners arriving in their territory, as they often come with baggage or alternative motives.

## Current events

Whispers of unrest are growing, even within the pristine walls of [[Dithoria City]]. Citizens have begun to distrust the government, which has led to increases of criminal activity in the city. Some believe the city's reliance on [[Dithoria]]'s protection is faltering, while others claim the Keeper's Council is growing corrupt. Meanwhile, the faithful work tirelessly to maintain their god's favor, even as their world continues to fracture around them.
