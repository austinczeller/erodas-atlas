[[Stormveil]] is a sleepy rainy logging town in the [[Pugent Delta]] region of [[Caldrith]]. There is folklore that the clouds in [[Stormveil]] are so thick they are seeing the underside of Nilah the storm dragon. Others think this is the birthplace of [[Parsus]].

Streets are muddy dirt roads with occasional horse drawn carts carrying timber and other goods. Many of the houses are log cabin style with small and sparse windows, with deep cherry shingles that guide the rain into bamboo-like funnels. The town square hosts the largest Stormwood tree, called Olde Reddy.

The town recently held the 33rd Veil-Master Race, its mayoral election. Numa won, with the support of the Miller's Union, over Terrance Highbrow, who wanted to expand the cuttings.
