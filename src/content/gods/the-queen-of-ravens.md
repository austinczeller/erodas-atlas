[[The Queen of Ravens]] is a lesser deity who grants mortals visions of the future. She grants them the power to see their deaths.

She has many servants across Erodas. In [[Korlornium]] she has a major temple at the World's Eye, although there have been rumors about secret underground temples and places of worship in the holy city of [[Dithoria City]], as people need comfort in the trying times.
