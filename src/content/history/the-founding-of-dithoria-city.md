[[Dithoria City]] was founded in the autumn of the year before [[The Great Wave]], during the month of Xhaale, by [[Edric Brightbar]], a half-orc and the first champion of [[Dithoria]]. He built the walls of the city from Silverstone, brilliant white rock quarried from [[Mount Redshield]] before its eruption and imbued by [[Dithoria]], to protect the people of the plains from [[The Great Wave]].

The city was raised on a hill at the foot of the mountains below [[Mount Redshield]], and Edric's Silverstone terraces lifted it higher still. The city rises in tiered levels, each built upon the one below, which are today the four rings of the city.

## The final brick

Dithorian tradition holds that Edric placed the final brick of the city's wall with his own hammer. The place where it was laid is marked today by a monument in the Mortar Gardens, in the Third Ring, with a view of the sea and [[Mount Redshield]]. His iconic pose, hammer in hand and placing that final brick, is repeated in the many statues of him that stand throughout the city.

## The Wave

When [[The Great Wave]] came the following year, much of the surrounding land was torn asunder, but the city's Silverstone walls remained unscathed and its people were sheltered. The people of [[Dithoria City]] believe their survival was not merely good fortune, but a testament to their faith and righteousness.

Edric's descendants became the Honored House, which today acts as the religious head of [[Dithoria]].
