[[Tidescrest]] is a festival celebrated in [[Thalmyre]] where a tournament is held to recruit newcomers to various piracy gangs, and establish power dynamics among groups. The main festivities occur on the Seat of Thalmyre.

The festival is everything the Seat of Thalmyre is when it tries to celebrate itself: greased masts, rum chugs, fire-breathers, goblin bookmakers, and the fighting pit.

The holiday was originally created to celebrate the [[Tidemother]], but this meaning is lost for most common folk, as pirates usually aren't too faithful.
