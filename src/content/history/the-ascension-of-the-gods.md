Seven years before [[The Great Wave]], the Pantheon of Major Powers ascended from Erodas to Yygarissil. Yygarissil is the common name for the place the gods ascend to. It is unknown whether this place exists in a parallel plane or outside of Erodas. It is believed that essences of life have come from this place, and that as the gods travel to different locations within Yygarissil, it creates natural phenomena which ripple onto Erodas.

The gods believed to reside within Yygarissil are [[Alune]], [[Elaris]], [[Forgas]], [[Parsus]], [[Sibuna]], [[Solwyn]], [[Sora]] and the [[Tidemother]].

## Those who remained

[[Ulthagos]] is said to have been cursed to carry the burden of sculpting the material world, and so he is not in Yygarissil.

Rumors note that [[Dithoria]] has not ascended to the heights of Yygarissil along with his kin. This would make him the only recognized Major Deity who has not taken the rite of ascension. It is unclear if these rumors are true, but it is common belief among the people of [[Dithoria City]] that he is roaming the lands of [[Siroth]] to bring order and justice to [[Korlornium]].

## The Severance

Ancient Dithorian scrolls suggest that [[The Severance]], held on the 24th of Salaale, falls around the time when the Pantheon of Major Powers exited Erodas. This meaning has long faded from common memory, and the Severance is celebrated today as a festival of feats and ingenuity.

The oldest known reference to [[Nhaalthurn]] also appears in fragments of Yygarissil's scripture, describing the gods themselves going silent on the eve of the world's second breath.
