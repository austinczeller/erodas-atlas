In this historic tournament on [[Thalmyre]], the Barnicle Boyz challenged the Blackwake Company for their seat on the Hydra, the piracy and crime syndicate of [[The Scar]]. The Blackwake Company were heavy favorites in the match, and speculation states that prior to the match the Barnicle Boyz successfully blackmailed Captain "Blackwake" Sobal to throw the match.

The tournament is held each year during [[Tidescrest]].
