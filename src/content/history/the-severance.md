[[The Severance]] occurs on the 24th of Salaale. It is celebrated today as a festival of feats and ingenuity. Events commonly occurring at Severance festivals include magic shows and competitions, travelling circuses, and fight pits.

[[The Severance]] is an ancient religious holiday whose meaning has been lost over time. It is believed through ancient Dithorian scrolls that it falls around the time when the Pantheon of Major Powers exited Erodas, but this meaning has long faded from common memory.
