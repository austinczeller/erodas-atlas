[[The Great Wave]] is known as a series of tsunamis and earthquakes which flooded the central lowlands of [[Korlornium]], creating [[The Scar]] as it is known today. A series of violent tectonic shifts beneath the continent triggered a devastating tsunami, swallowing entire cities and drowning vast stretches of fertile land beneath a relentless surge of water.

As if the wrath of the earth itself was not enough, the upheaval awakened [[Mount Redshield]], a long-dormant volcano that erupted with terrifying force. Molten rock spilled down its slopes while a massive plume of soot and ash blotted out the sky, casting [[Korlornium]] into an unnatural twilight for months. Crops failed, rivers ran black with debris, and the air itself became thick with the scent of fire and decay.

## The lost lowlands

Prior to [[The Great Wave]], much of [[Korlornium]]'s people lived in settlements in the central lowlands of the continent, therefore much of the written records of [[The Great Wave]] were lost. However, pockets of communities were able to survive this catastrophe as they escaped toward high ground.

Survivors speak of the great temples and cities lost beneath the waves, their ruins now hidden within the depths of [[The Scar]]. Among them were entire cities built in the name of [[Ulthagos]], and some say the surge was sent by the [[Tidemother]] herself in response to the land's dominance. It is whispered that some of those who perished in the flood never truly died, their spirits lingering in the drowned ruins, restless and vengeful. In [[Korlornium]] many people believe the Sea-folk are the undead souls of those who died in the Wave. Others believe that powerful relics, artifacts of the old gods, were buried beneath the silt and stone, waiting to be unearthed.

## The walls of Dithoria

Survivors speculated that [[The Great Wave]] was an act of the gods. It is a common sentiment that the civilizations residing in the central lowlands were meddling in arcane powers beyond the gods' wishes.

In [[Caldrith]], the survival of [[Dithoria City]] is remembered as the great exception. Edric Brightbar, the first champion of [[Dithoria]], built the walls of the city from Silverstone, rock of [[Mount Redshield]] imbued by [[Dithoria]], to protect the people of the plains from the Wave. While much of the surrounding land was torn asunder, the city's walls remained unscathed. The people of [[Dithoria City]] believe their survival was not merely good fortune, but a testament to their faith and righteousness.

## Afterward

[[The Great Wave]] did more than just reshape the land. It shattered faiths, destroyed kingdoms, and forced entire civilizations to adapt to a harsher, more unforgiving world. The once-thriving lowlands became a chaotic expanse of shifting waters, jagged islands, and drowned ruins, a lawless frontier where desperate survivors, exiled scholars, and ruthless pirates now make their home.

The Erodian Standard Calendar counts its years from the year after the creation of [[The Scar]]. Years prior are referred to as negative numbers, or through the term Pre-Scar. The time following [[The Great Wave]] is usually referred to as [[The Red Storm]].
