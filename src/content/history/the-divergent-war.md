[[The Divergent War]] refers to the war which followed [[The Red Storm]]. It was named the Divergent War because it politically separated [[Siroth]] and [[Caldrith]].

The war was between the religious entities of [[Dithoria City]], the militia of the Righteous Banner, and the Rellian Dynasty, the power in control of [[Siroth]] at the time.

## The Ascendants

Dithorian histories record that the Rellian Dynasty created powerful weapons, known as the Ascendants, in order to take over [[Caldrith]]. The Ascendant Ritual was performed with the assistance of [[Goghdor]] magic, which granted immortality and superhuman powers to the worthy. Not all were capable of wielding such power, and many fell to corruption. In modern texts the Ascendants are framed as power hungry people who used an unnatural and unholy magic to gain power.

In [[Siroth]], the Rellian Dynasty is remembered for its ideals of equality, and for wishing to cease the exploitation of [[Siroth]]'s natural resources and magical ley lines.

## The fighting

Most of the fighting took place on the land bridge near [[Konduit]], where [[Caldrith]] and [[Siroth]] still meet in the north. The Righteous Banner was rallied by [[Dithoria City]] and blessed by [[Dithoria]] to keep his kingdom safe. It was commanded by Hethul, the Righteous, a mighty goliath warrior who wielded the Lawbearer's Hammer.

The war came to a conclusion as religious forces from [[Dithoria City]] pushed the Rellian troops out of [[Wyegate]]. At the Battle of [[Wyegate]], the Righteous Banner defeated much of the Rellian troops and united northern [[Caldrith]] under its banner. Dithorian texts say the magic of [[Dithoria]] was able to cleanse the Ascendants of their power.

## Afterward

The Rellian Dynasty did not survive the war. The Righteous Banner still controls much of [[Caldrith]]'s northeastern land, except for the free city of [[Konduit]], which remains a religious and political neutral point between [[Dithoria City]] and [[Siroth]]. The Lawbearer's Hammer is held today by the Keeper's Council in [[Dithoria City]].
