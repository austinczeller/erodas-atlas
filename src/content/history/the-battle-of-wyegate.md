The Battle of [[Wyegate]] was the battle that brought [[The Divergent War]] to a conclusion. It was fought over three days in the winter of Year 41, from the 18th to the 20th of Salaale, on the plains of [[Caldrith]] at [[Wyegate]], on the coast of [[The Scar]] just south of [[Dithoria City]].

The Righteous Banner, the military brigade of [[Dithoria City]], was commanded in the battle by [[Hethul, the Righteous]], a mighty goliath warrior who wielded the Lawbearer's Hammer. The Righteous Banner defeated much of the Rellian troops at [[Wyegate]] and pushed the troops of the Rellian Dynasty out of [[Caldrith]].

## Afterward

The victory at [[Wyegate]] united northern [[Caldrith]] under the Righteous Banner, and the war was over by the turn of the new year. The Righteous Banner still controls much of [[Caldrith]]'s northeastern land today, except for the free city of [[Konduit]]. The Lawbearer's Hammer is held today by the Keeper's Council within [[Dithoria City]].

In [[Dithoria City]] the battle is remembered as the moment the war was won.
