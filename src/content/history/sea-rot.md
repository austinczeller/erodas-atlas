The [[Sea Rot]] was a curse afflicting [[Duskwind Landing]]. Sailors who left the harbour returned with blistering skin and fever-clouded minds, or didn't return at all. Trade slowed, the town grew tense, and rumours of dark magic spread through the docks.

A wizard hiding on Widow's Rook was responsible, using the curse to hoard Spice shipments and disrupt commerce. Around the same time, a secretive group of gnomes arrived in town, watching, quiet, and seemingly connected to something larger.

A travelling band of adventurers investigated and cleansed the town of the affliction, and goods have now begun to flow again.
