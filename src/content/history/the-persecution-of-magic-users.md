For a time after [[The Great Wave]], magic users were persecuted in [[Siroth]]. Many took shelter in [[Dithoria City]], where they believe that magic, specifically divine magic from the god of law and order, [[Dithoria]], saved them behind the walls of the great city from the tragedies which plagued the rest of the continent. This persecution has shaped the [[Siroth]] culture to be very conservative.

The Fowlmen were among those hunted. In ancient times, they thrived as nomadic clans, soaring over dunes and navigating the shifting sands with an instinctual mastery of wind currents. They were deeply revered as seers and scholars, their wisdom sought by desert dwellers. During the persecution, many Fowlmen were hunted, driven from their ancestral lands, or slain. Some managed to flee deep into the Southern Siroth Desert, vanishing into the endless sands, while others perished defending their right to wield magic. Few escaped to [[Dithoria City]], as they have a deep connection to the land and the skies of [[Siroth]].

Today, sightings of Fowlmen are rare, and their surviving enclaves remain hidden. Those who still roam the world often do so in secret, wary of outsiders yet still carrying the ancient knowledge of their people.
