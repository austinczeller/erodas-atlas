The 33rd Veil-Master Race was the mayoral race of the town of [[Stormveil]]. It followed the death of the previous Veilfather, Damon Warthelm, who was found dead in the Permitting Office of Stormveil. Many folks were suspicious of his death.

The candidates were Terrance Highbrow and Numa. Highbrow wanted to remove previous logging restrictions in the [[Heaven's Hole]] area and gained quick popularity, as the town was looking to boost the economy.

During Highbrow's speech, an attack by doppelgangers interrupted the election, and the town conducted its vote at the Soggy Bottom afterward. The winner was Numa, with Rick Augerland as the vice-master, with the support of the Miller's Union.
