[[Edric Brightbar]] was a half-orc and the first champion of [[Dithoria]]. He is known as the founder of [[Dithoria City]]. He built the walls of the city from Silverstone, rock of [[Mount Redshield]] imbued by [[Dithoria]], to protect the people of the plains from [[The Great Wave]].

Many statues of [[Edric Brightbar]] stand throughout [[Dithoria City]]. His iconic pose shows him with his hammer, placing the final brick of the city's wall. A monument stands in the Mortar Gardens where this final brick was laid.

The Honored House of [[Dithoria City]] are his direct descendants.
