[[Malroy Brightbar]] leads the Honored House, the direct descendants of [[Edric Brightbar]], founder of [[Dithoria City]].

The Honored House, otherwise known as the Holy House, is a part of the Keeper's Council and acts as the religious head of [[Dithoria]]. It is recognized as the most powerful group in [[Korlornium]].
