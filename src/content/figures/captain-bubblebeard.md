[[Captain Bubblebeard]] was a large, fearsome pirate and leader of the Stray Dogs, one of the oldest piracy groups in [[Korlornium]], based on the northernmost island of [[Thalmyre]]. He was a tall, scarred bugbear with one glass eye and a long beard he often spilled his ale in.

He fought with the energy of someone who had never seriously considered losing and the warmth of someone who shook every hand after he knocked its owner down. He was found dead in his chair during the most recent Tidescrest festival.
