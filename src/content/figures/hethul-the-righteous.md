[[Hethul, the Righteous]] was a mighty goliath warrior and a leader of old of the Righteous Banner, the military brigade of [[Dithoria City]]. He commanded its troops during [[The Divergent War]] and led them in the [[Battle of Wyegate]], where the Righteous Banner defeated much of the Rellian troops.

He wielded a large and mighty weapon, the Lawbearer's Hammer, which is held today by the Keeper's Council within [[Dithoria City]].
