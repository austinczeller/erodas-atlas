[[Hitchcock 'Goldentooth' Derose]] is the smooth talking captain of the Murky Bellows, a classic piracy gang and one of the founding members of the Hydra, the syndicate that controls much of the shipping in [[Korlornium]].

The Murky Bellows operate out of [[Thalmyre]], with their headquarters on the Seat of Thalmyre, and control many of the trade routes out of [[Dithoria City]] through [[The Scar]]. They appear welcoming at first and willing to help, at a cost. Many wealthy merchants recommend them, as they are mostly honest when they are cheating you.
