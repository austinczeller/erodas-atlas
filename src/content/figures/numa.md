[[Numa]] is a human woman residing in [[Stormveil]]. She left the community at [[Heaven's Hole]] to protect their way of life, and ran for Veil-Mother against Terrance Highbrow to protect [[Heaven's Hole]] from logging.

She won the 33rd Veil-Master Race and partnered with Rick Augerland and the Miller's Union to ensure economic prosperity in [[Stormveil]]. She is out of her element in [[Stormveil]] but is passionate about helping others.
