[[Antiochus]] is the champion of [[Sibuna]], goddess of death. In the early days of mortality within the Mundane World, [[Sibuna]] acknowledged him as worthy enough to take as her champion.

He was known as the first mortal to acknowledge death and the wolf who hunts our souls, and he was fascinated by the afterlife.
