[[Captain Garrick Hollowmaw]], the Dread Pirate, is a legend of [[The Scar]]. The day he died is still celebrated across [[Korlornium]] as the [[Festival of Tides]], which originated on [[Thalmyre]].

Occasionally brave pirates have attempted to find his bounties, rumored to be located on one of the islands of [[Thalmyre]].
