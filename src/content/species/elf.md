Elves are a long living humanoid intelligent species. They are one of the most widespread species in [[Korlornium]] alongside [[Human|humans]].

It is rumored that the elves encountered elsewhere in Erodas, including those in [[Korlornium]], are descendants of those who left [[Seivara]] long ago, though the reasons for their departure are not agreed upon. In the countryside of [[Wyegate]], the Silvertree Conservatory teaches half-elves the proper Elven culture.

The days of the Erodian Standard Calendar are each named for an Elvish element, from Vitael, the spirit, to Vaelchron, time.
