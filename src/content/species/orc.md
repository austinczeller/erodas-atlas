Orcs are hardy, tough and large individuals. They are similar to [[Dwarf|dwarves]] as they take pride in craft and muscle. Notable features include small tusks which grow from their lower jaw, and a pale to dark green complexion.

The founder of [[Dithoria City]], [[Edric Brightbar]], was a half-orc.
