Sea-folk are a species of intelligent humanoids present in coastal regions around Erodas. They commonly have bluish pale skin, often with mollusks, clams and starfish on them, and are often large in stature.

It is believed that they are spawned from the love between the [[Tidemother]] and [[Alune]]. Legends state that [[Alune]]'s sister [[Sibuna]] granted the couple the gift of a child, which is now known as the Sea-folk.

In [[Korlornium]] many people believe that Sea-folk are the undead souls of those who died in [[The Great Wave]]. They are fairly common in areas around [[The Scar]].
