---
title: Dwarf
rarity: Common
description: Master crafters of iron and stone, found throughout Korlornium's trade cities.
order: 3
---
Dwarves are renowned for their skill in crafting, particularly with iron, stone, and other durable materials. They are expert blacksmiths, stoneworkers, and engineers, known for creating intricate designs and durable constructions that can withstand the test of time. Their societies are built on the principles of craftsmanship, tradition, and the pursuit of perfection in their works.

Dwarves have a deep connection to the gods [[Ulthagos]], the god of the land and earth, and [[Forgas]], the god of fire and forge.

Dwarves typically make their homes in elaborate caverns and caves, often located deep within the mountains or rolling hills. These subterranean dwellings are marvels of engineering, with stone halls and chambers intricately carved into the earth. Many of their cities have sprawling networks of tunnels and mines, where dwarves extract precious metals, gems, and stone.

Although dwarves are primarily associated with the mountains and hills, they are not confined to these places. Dwarves can be found throughout [[Korlornium]], particularly in bustling trade cities or areas where their skills are in high demand. They have earned a reputation for being shrewd and meticulous traders.

Dwarves live by a strict code of honor and tradition. They value loyalty, hard work, and respect for their ancestors and gods. Their society is often organized in clans, with clan leaders usually chosen based on merit and achievement in craftsmanship, battle, or leadership. Though dwarves are known for their stoic and serious demeanor, their halls are filled with laughter, song, and the clinking of hammers and anvils, and dwarven feasts are famous for their hearty food, fine mead, and the sharing of tales passed down through generations.
