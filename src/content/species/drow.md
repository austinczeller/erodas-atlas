title: Drow
rarity: Common

order: 4
---
Drow are a sub-species of elves which have historically been persecuted and dwelled in societies underground. Their skin is a deep to light purple or blue.

Today drow are commonly found above or below ground, in settlements and cities alike. The ancient remains of drow cities are said to be lost with time.
