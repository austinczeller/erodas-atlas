The next campaign begins in [[Dithoria City]], the largest city in [[Korlornium]] and the seat of the church of [[Dithoria]], god of law and order. Read the [[Dithoria City]] entry before you build your character. Everything on this page is something your character could know.

## The city right now

Whispers of unrest are growing inside the city's white walls. Citizens have begun to distrust the government, and criminal activity is on the rise. Some believe the city's reliance on [[Dithoria]]'s protection is faltering, while others claim the Keeper's Council is growing corrupt. Strange creatures have been reported in and around the city, and no one in authority will say where they come from.

Magic is the dividing line. The church teaches that all magic is the power of [[Dithoria]], and the Keeper's Council restricts its use within the walls. Only wizards certified by the Arcane Order of Dithoria may practice it, and the Arcane Order, who act as the city's police, check the permits of everyone from merchants to passers-by. Most citizens no longer believe magic belongs to the god, but some fundamentalists still do, and the argument splits families, guilds and congregations.

## What makes a good character for this campaign

**Your character wants to solve a problem.** Not a vague destiny, but something concrete they can work toward: a debt to repay, a person to find, a law to change, a name to clear, a stall to win back. The campaign will move fastest when your character has a reason to act, not just a reason to follow.

**Your character has a reason to be in Dithoria City right now.** They might have grown up in one of its rings, arrived this season looking for work, been sent by someone, or be stuck here. Decide which ring they live or work in. In Dithoria City your address is your standing: someone from the Coin Ring is treated very differently from someone from the Wash.

**Your character has a relationship with the law.** Every adult in the city carries identification and personal permits. Decide whether your character has theirs in order, what they would need a permit for, and how they feel about the Arcane Order. If your character casts spells, decide whether they are certified.

**Your character has an opinion about Dithoria.** Faithful, doubting, indifferent or openly hostile, it will matter. The church is everywhere in the city, and its power over daily life is the source of much of the current tension.

## Questions to answer before the first session

1. What problem does your character want to solve, and what is stopping them?
2. Why are they in Dithoria City, and which ring do they call home (or sleep in)?
3. Who in the city do they trust, and who do they owe?
4. Do they use magic? If so, do they have a permit, and what happens if they are caught without one?
5. What do they believe about [[Dithoria]], and has that belief been tested?
6. What do they want the city to look like when this is all over?

## Character seeds

These are starting points, not requirements. Take one, combine two, or ignore them all.

- **The unlicensed.** You can cast magic, but you have no certification from the Arcane Order. Every spell you cast in the city is a crime. You have a reason to keep using it anyway.
- **The newly certified.** You passed the Arcane Order's certification, or you have just joined its ranks. The permits you are asked to check belong to people you grew up with.
- **The doubting faithful.** You were raised in the church of [[Dithoria]], perhaps as an acolyte or the child of a priest. You still pray, but the city's arguments over magic have shaken you, and you want to know what is true.
- **The fallen merchant.** Your family held a stall on the High Row of the Marble Market for generations, until this season's inspection by the Keeper's Council took it away. You intend to get it back, and you have questions about why it was taken.
- **Born in the Wash.** You grew up in the Fourth Ring, near the harbor, among dockhands, sailors and outsiders. You know how the city treats people from the bottom ring, and you know which doors in the Wash stay open after the last bell.
- **From across the Scar.** You came from [[Siroth]] or the isles of [[Thalmyre]] looking for a better life, and found suspicion, tariffs and a stack of permits instead. Fowlmen especially are rare in the city and draw attention.
- **The Silvertree student.** You are a half-elf from the Silvertree Conservatory in the countryside of [[Wyegate]], sent to the city on an errand for the school, and you have stayed longer than you were supposed to.
- **The Banner veteran.** You served in the Righteous Banner, the military brigade of Dithoria City that still holds northeastern [[Caldrith]]. You know the stories of [[The Divergent War]] better than most, and you have seen things on patrol that the stories do not explain.
- **Dragonborn of the city.** Your family left [[Dracofold]] generations ago and settled in [[Dithoria City]]. You still keep a quiet version of the Ashen Communion, and you are tired of being looked at as if the dragons sent you.
- **The farmhand from Wyegate.** The farmland between [[Wyegate]] and the city feeds much of [[Dithoria City]]. You came into the city to sell, to argue, or to find someone, and something here has kept you from going home.
- **The scholar of the Volley.** Dithorian scholars have recorded [[The Astral Volley]] for centuries. You work in those archives, and you have begun to wonder what, exactly, the gods are shooting at.

## Notes on character creation

The campaign uses the 2024 rules of Dungeons and Dragons. Any species from the Player's Handbook is welcome, but consider how common your people are in [[Korlornium]]. The [[Korlornium]] entry describes the peoples of the continent. Fowlmen and Star Touched Aasimar from [[Goghdor]] are rare in the city and will be noticed. Talk to your DM about anything that would tie your character to the places and people of the atlas.
